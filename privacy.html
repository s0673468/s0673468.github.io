rental-reports privacy policy

Last updated 17 October 2026

rental-reports is a private tool used by one person, the owner of this site, on their own computer. It is not offered to anyone else.

What it accesses

Read-only access to Google Drive (drive.readonly) for the signed-in owner. It uses that access only to check when one spreadsheet chosen by the owner last changed, and to download a copy of it.

Where data goes

Downloaded files and the access token stay on the owner's own computer; the token is stored in the macOS Keychain. Nothing is sent to any other server, shared, sold, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Retention

Files are kept locally until the owner deletes them.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Limited Use

rental-reports' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
