Food Blob privacy policy

Effective 27 October 2026. Applies to Food Blob for Android (package com.german.foodblob).

Food Blob is published by Germano Cher. It is a local food diary. It has no account, advertising, analytics, tracking, cloud sync or backend. Food Blob does not collect your data or send it to the developer. The Android app has no internet permission.

Information on your device

Daily dates, your green, yellow and red counts, preferences, and the records needed to apply or undo local actions stay in Food Blob's private storage on your device. Home Screen widgets show your local counts. Food Blob does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or advertising identifier, and it does not send push notifications.

On Android, backup and device-transfer rules exclude Food Blob's stored data. Keep your own export if you want a copy before changing phones.

Export and import

Export writes a copy only when you ask for it, to a destination you pick in the system document picker. That destination may be run by another app or cloud provider, and its privacy policy applies to the copy you put there. Import reads only a file you pick. Food Blob does not upload these files itself.

No other app can read Food Blob's records on your phone.

Keeping and deleting your data

Records stay on your device until you delete them or remove the app. Settings has export and Delete All Data controls; Delete All Data also removes saved recovery copies. Copies you exported earlier are separate and must be deleted where you saved them. There is no server account or server copy for the developer to delete.

Health information

Food Blob is a personal food-logging and reflection tool. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any medical condition, and it does not give medical or nutritional advice. Always consult a healthcare professional for medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

Children

Food Blob is intended for adults and is not directed at children.

Changes

If Food Blob's data practices change, this policy will be updated before the matching release, and the date above will change.
